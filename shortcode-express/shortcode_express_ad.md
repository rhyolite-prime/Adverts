# ShortCode Express — “Build Beyond the Smartphone”

**40-second advert:** Bold, cinematic, engineer-focused  
*Approximately 100 spoken words. Start tense; shift to confident momentum at the reveal.*

| Time | Voiceover | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|
| **0–7 sec** | “Africa moves money with a dial. But while apps got frameworks, USSD got endless if-else.” | A customer dials a USSD code. Cut to an engineer facing tangled conditional logic. Music builds tension. |
| **7–11 sec** | “Meet ShortCode Express. Powered by Sapo Engine.” | The code untangles into a clean visual workflow. Brand reveal; music opens up. |
| **11–19 sec** | “Get a short code—or bring yours. Drag. Drop. Connect. Build USSD flows without writing code.” | Select a short code. Drag menus onto the canvas. Connect branches. A working flow appears on a phone. |
| **19–27 sec** | “Connect mobile money, banking, SMS, and KYC through the plugin marketplace. Need something unique? Build your own plugin.” | Service tiles snap into the flow. Additional labels flash: **OTP · Vouchers · Commerce · Logistics.** |
| **27–33 sec** | “Simulate. Validate. Deploy. Track sessions. Roll back with confidence.” | Rapid cuts: phone simulator, validated build, release button, analytics, rollback. |
| **33–40 sec** | “Less branching chaos. More control. Build beyond the smartphone. ShortCode Express. Be fluent.” | The workflow becomes a live customer interaction. Resolve to the logo and CTA: **Build your first flow.** |
